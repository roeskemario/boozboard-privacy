BoozBoard Support We're here to help. Find answers below or get in touch. Privacy Policy · Terms of Use Frequently Asked Questions How do I join a group? Open BoozBoard, tap "Join Group" on the home screen, and enter the 4-letter code shared by the group creator. No account needed. How do I create a group? Tap "Create Group" on the home screen, enter a group name, and share the generated 4-letter code with your friends. What is The Vault? The Vault is your personal history of past nights. When a night ends, it's saved automatically with stats, photos, and a share card. Free accounts save up to 10 nights. Vault Pass saves unlimited nights forever. How do I cancel my Vault Pass subscription? Open the Settings app on your iPhone → tap your Apple ID at the top → Subscriptions → BoozBoard → Cancel Subscription. You can also manage it at appleid.apple.com. How do I restore a previous Vault Pass purchase? Open BoozBoard → tap "Get Pro" → tap "Restore Purchases" at the bottom of the paywall screen. Are BAC readings accurate? BAC figures in BoozBoard are mathematical estimates based on drinks logged, weight, and time. They are for awareness purposes only and are not medically accurate. Never use them to determine whether it is safe to drive. How do I delete a night from The Vault? Open The Vault, swipe left on the night you want to delete, and tap "Delete." I lost my Vault after reinstalling the app. Can I get it back? Currently, Vault data is stored locally on your device. If you deleted the app or switched phones, the data cannot be recovered.
