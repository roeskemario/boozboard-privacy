BoozBoard

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Overview

BoozBoard helps groups of friends track a shared night out in real time. To make live syncing and cloud backup possible, some data is transmitted to and stored on Firebase (Google's cloud platform). This policy explains exactly what is stored, where, and for how long.

What Goes to Firebase — Active Sessions

When you create or join a group, the following is sent to Firebase Firestore for the duration of that session only:

  • The group code and group name
  • Your display name and when you joined
  • The drinks you log (label, icon, volume, ABV, timestamp)
  • Photos you add during the night (uploaded to Firebase Storage)

Session data in Firestore is automatically deleted the moment the group host ends the night. Your body weight and biological sex are never sent to Firebase — they stay on your device only and are used solely to estimate your BAC locally.

What Goes to Firebase — Vault Backup

When a night ends, a summary of that session (group name, drink log, and photos) is backed up to Firebase Firestore under a randomly generated Vault ID. This ID is a random string of characters with no connection to your name, email, phone number, or any real-world identity.

This backup allows you to restore your nights on a new device by entering your Vault code. You can view your Vault ID in the Profile tab at any time.

Friends you add are stored locally on your device only and are never uploaded to any server.

What Stays on Your Device Only

The following is stored only in your phone's local storage:

  • Your body weight and biological sex (for BAC estimates)
  • Your personal profile (name, weight, sex) for auto-fill convenience
  • Your friends list (names and their Vault codes)
  • Your saved crews

Deleting the app permanently removes all locally stored data.

Photos

Photos added during a session are uploaded to Firebase Storage and are accessible to all members of that group. Photos are not automatically deleted after the night ends. Do not upload photos you would not want other group members to see. If you need a photo removed, contact us with the group code.

Subscriptions

BoozBoard Pro subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Store). We do not store or process your payment details. Subscription status is verified through the respective platform's billing system. For billing support, contact Apple or Google directly.

Firebase and Google

BoozBoard uses Firebase, operated by Google LLC. Data stored through Firebase is subject to Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Group codes and Vault IDs are random identifiers — they are not linked to any account, email address, phone number, or real-world identity.

BAC Estimates

BoozBoard calculates estimated Blood Alcohol Content (BAC) using the Widmark formula. This is a mathematical estimate for general awareness only — not a medical measurement and not a substitute for a certified breathalyzer. Never use BAC estimates in this app to make decisions about driving, operating machinery, or any other safety-critical activity.

Children

BoozBoard is not intended for users under the legal drinking age in their jurisdiction (21 in the United States). We do not knowingly collect information from minors. If you believe a minor has used this app, please delete it from their device immediately.

Your Rights

  • Local data: uninstall the app at any time to delete everything stored on your device.
  • Session data: automatically deleted from Firebase when the night ends.
  • Vault backup: contact us with your Vault ID to request deletion of your backed-up nights.
  • Photos: contact us with the group code to request removal of specific photos.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us through the app's listing on the App Store. We will update this policy whenever our data practices change.
